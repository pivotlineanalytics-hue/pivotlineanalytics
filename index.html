About the Founder

Glinn Co. LLC was founded with a simple purpose: to support business owners and strengthen the communities they serve. Pivot Line Analytics is one branch of that mission, focused on turning data into clarity, confidence, and informed decision making.

Through years of watching American businesses confront and overcome significant economic challenges, we developed a respect for the resilience that drives meaningful growth. That experience inspired our commitment to help business owners build strong, informed, and durable companies positioned for long-term success.

With a strong background in business analytics and real-world leadership experience, we help owners focus on what matters most and operate with clarity instead of guesswork.

At Pivot Line Analytics, we understand that most business owners do not have the time or the resources to dig into their data. We help turn data into clarity and time into growth, giving owners support they can count on and decisions they can trust.
